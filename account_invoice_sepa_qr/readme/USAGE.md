Nothing to do: confirm an invoice and print or send it. The code appears below the payment reference of every open
invoice in euro whose bank account is an active IBAN account of your own company (a SEPA country).

No code is printed for paid invoices, for other currencies, for non-SEPA accounts and when the bank account of the
invoice belongs to somebody else. Switch it off in *Accounting ‣ Settings ‣ Customer Payments ‣ Display SEPA QR-code*.
